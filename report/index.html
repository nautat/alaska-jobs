Methods

Where the numbers come from

Each figure is built from public data by a small Python pipeline. Different sources count different things, so they don't always agree. Here is what each one sees and misses.

  • Wage-and-salary jobs (BLS QCEW, 2014–2025). Jobs covered by unemployment insurance, counted where the job is. It leaves out the self-employed, most commercial fishermen, and active-duty military. Small industries in small boroughs are suppressed for confidentiality and shown as gaps, never as zero. 2025 has unusually heavy suppression, so 2024 is the snapshot year.
  • All jobs, including self-employed and military (BEA CAEMP25N, 2001–2022). Full- and part-time jobs by place of work, including proprietors.
  • Personal income (BEA CAINC4, CAINC35, through 2024 / 2022). Wages, business income, investment income, and government transfers by borough. The Permanent Fund Dividend sits in "other transfers from government."
  • What residents do (Census ACS 5-year, 2020–2024). A survey of where people live, not where they work. It includes the self-employed. Small places have wide margins of error, shown as ± values at 90% confidence.
  • Businesses by ZIP code (Census ZIP Business Patterns, 2023). Establishments with paid employees, counted in March.
  • Commercial fishing (Alaska CFEC, 2023). Permit holders and estimated gross earnings by where the fisherman lives.
  • Permanent Fund Dividend (Alaska Dept. of Revenue). Amount paid per person, 1982–2025.
  • Boundaries (Census cartographic files, 2023). The 30 boroughs and census areas, plus CDPs, roads, rail, and rivers for the Upper Susitna map.
